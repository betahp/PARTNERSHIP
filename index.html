A note for Adam

Welcome, my love!

I created this space to hold the care and the seriousness with which I understand this unusual union carries. I hope that here you can open your heart, because far beyond the immigration process, this will become a great treasure for us to keep with pride — this union that has been woven with so much beauty.

For your letter to hold weight as evidence in our partnership visa application, we ask that you include:

  • How our relationship came to be, from your perspective — how we met and how it grew
  • How you've seen us build our life together, day by day
  • Concrete examples of our commitment to each other — the hard moments we've faced, the plans we're making, the ordinary days that hold us together
  • What this relationship means to you, in your own words
  • Your full name and the date you wrote it
  • A line confirming that everything you wrote is true

It doesn't need to be formal — write it the way you'd talk to me.

A note from us

Welcome, and thank you

Welcome to the story of this unusual and blessed union we're building together. Thank you for being such an important part of this journey.

For your letter to hold weight as evidence in our partnership visa application, we ask that you include:

  • How you know us, and for how long (your relationship to Roberta and/or Adam)
  • How you've seen our relationship develop — when you first noticed it, memorable moments, changes you've witnessed
  • Concrete examples you've personally seen of our commitment to each other (trips, support during hard times, everyday life together, plans we make together)
  • Your honest opinion on the authenticity of our relationship
  • Your full name, a contact detail, and the date you wrote it
  • A line confirming that everything you wrote is true, in your own words

It doesn't need to be formal — write it the way you'd normally talk to us. What matters most is that it's genuine and specific, with details only someone who truly knows us would be able to share.
